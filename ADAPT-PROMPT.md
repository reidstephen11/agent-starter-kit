# Point your agent at this repo

This is the prompt for the **adapt** path — you want a setup that fits your teaching, your
tools and your school, and this repo is raw material rather than a thing to install. It
assumes nothing about which AI tool you use.

Clone or download this repo somewhere, open your agent in that folder (or attach/upload
the files if your tool works that way), and paste everything below the line.

If instead you want to *use* the structure more or less as it stands, use
`SETUP-PROMPT.md` — it configures the kit itself rather than designing you a new one.

---

You are helping me design an agent setup for my own teaching. This folder contains
someone else's working setup, published as a reference. Treat it as evidence of what one
teacher found out, not as a specification to install.

Read these first, in this order, before you do anything else:

1. `LEARNINGS.md` — what went wrong for them and what it cost. This is the important file.
2. `README.md` — what the setup is and how it is put together.
3. `workflows/curriculum-pass.md` and `workflows/feedback-pass.md` — the two procedures.
4. `AGENTS.md` and `Context/index.md` — the standing instructions and the context pack.

Then work with me on **my** setup, under these conditions:

- **Design for my tools, not theirs.** Ask me what AI tool I use, where my files live, what
  my LMS is, what my curriculum authority is, and what my school's position on AI is.
  Their answers to all of those are in the repo and are almost certainly not mine.
- **Assume I will not copy their folder structure.** Start from what I already have and
  what I actually want to do. Bring across only the things from `LEARNINGS.md` that apply
  to the job I've described — and tell me which ones you're leaving out and why.
- **Do not fill anything in with your guesses about my school, my cohort or my beliefs.**
  A context pack you wrote by inference is worse than an empty one, because I will stop
  being able to tell which of the standards in it are mine. Ask me; write down what I
  actually said; do not inflate a one-line answer into three paragraphs.
- **One question at a time**, and wait for my answer.
- **Say plainly if you think something I'm asking for is a bad idea**, before you build it.

Start by asking me one question: **what is the job I actually want an agent to do?** Then
tell me, from `LEARNINGS.md`, which of that teacher's findings bear on it and which are
irrelevant to me — before you propose any structure at all.

Do not draft any teaching resource, unit, lesson or student feedback in this session. This
session is for designing the setup.

Two things from their setup that I want you to argue for or against explicitly, rather than
silently adopting:

- **A context pack** — a small set of files stating what good means in my context, that
  you read instead of improvising from general educational knowledge.
- **A hard separation** between planning work and any folder containing student work.

If either doesn't fit my situation, say so and say what should replace it.
